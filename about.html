About Us & Our Faith

Anchored in Scripture, compelled by love, and dedicated to the glory of God.

Our Mission

To glorify God by making devoted followers of Jesus Christ who worship wholeheartedly, grow together in authentic biblical community, and serve our neighborhood and the nations with sacrificial love.

Our Vision

To see Springfield transformed by the hope and restorative power of the Gospel—a church where the weary find rest, the hurting find healing, and believers are equipped for everyday ministry.

Doctrinal Foundations

Statement of Faith

These foundational truths represent our understanding of Holy Scripture. In essential beliefs we have unity, in non-essential beliefs we have liberty, and in all things we show love.

1. The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible—consisting of the sixty-six books of the Old and New Testaments—is the fully inspired Word of God, without error in the original manuscripts. It is the complete revelation of God’s will for human salvation and the supreme and final authority for all Christian faith, life, and doctrine.

Scripture References: 2 Timothy 3:16-17; 2 Peter 1:20-21; Psalm 119:105; Matthew 5:18

2. The One True God

We believe in one eternal, personal, and infinite God, who created all things visible and invisible. He exists eternally in three distinct persons—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—equal in divine power, perfection, and nature, while executing distinct yet harmonious offices in the great work of redemption.

Scripture References: Deuteronomy 6:4; Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Genesis 1:1

3. The Lord Jesus Christ

We believe that Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, truly God and truly man. Conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary, He lived a sinless life, died a substitutionary death on the cross for our sins, arose bodily from the dead on the third day, ascended into heaven where He reigns at the Father’s right hand as our High Priest, and will personally return in glory.

Scripture References: John 1:1-14; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4; Philippians 2:5-11; Hebrews 4:14-16; Titus 2:13

4. The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is the third person of the Trinity. He convicts the world of sin, righteousness, and judgment. At the moment of salvation, He regenerates, indwells, seals, and unites every believer to Christ. He comforts, guides, equips believers with spiritual gifts, and empowers them to live holy lives and witness boldly.

Scripture References: John 14:16-17, 26; John 16:7-11; Romans 8:9-11; Galatians 5:22-25; 1 Corinthians 12:4-11

5. Human Fall & Salvation by Grace

We believe humanity was created innocent and in the image of God, but fell into sin through voluntary disobedience. Consequently, all human beings are born with a sinful nature and are separated from God. Salvation cannot be earned through human effort, religious rituals, or good works; it is the free gift of God’s grace received solely through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ and His finished work on the cross.

Scripture References: Genesis 1:26-27; Romans 3:23; Romans 5:12; Ephesians 2:8-10; Titus 3:5; John 3:16

6. The Church & The Ordinances

We believe the Church is the spiritual body of Christ, of which He is the head. It consists of all born-again believers worldwide and expresses itself through local gatherings for worship, fellowship, prayer, and the proclamation of the Gospel. We practice two ordinances instituted by Christ: Believer's Baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith, and the Lord’s Supper (Communion) as a sacred remembrance of Christ's sacrifice.

Scripture References: Matthew 16:18; Ephesians 1:22-23; Acts 2:41-47; Matthew 28:19-20; 1 Corinthians 11:23-26

7. The Blessed Hope & Eternity

We believe in the personal, imminent, and glorious return of our Lord Jesus Christ. We believe in the bodily resurrection of both the saved and the unsaved—the saved unto eternal life, joy, and unbroken fellowship with God in the new heavens and new earth, and the unsaved unto eternal separation from God.

Scripture References: 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18; Revelation 20:11-15; Revelation 21:1-5; John 5:28-29
