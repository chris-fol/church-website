First Time Visiting?

What to Expect on a Sunday Morning

Visiting a small country church for the first time is comfortable and relaxed. Here is what you can look forward to:

Friendly Greetings

You will be greeted at the door by warm smiles and a handshake. There are no strangers here for long.

Traditional & Heartfelt Worship

We sing cherished traditional hymns along with simple praise songs, led together as a congregation.

Biblical Teaching

Straightforward, verse-by-verse messages from the Bible that are practical, encouraging, and centered on Jesus Christ.

Come As You Are

You'll see everything from blue jeans and work boots to casual slacks and Sunday dresses. Come comfortable.
