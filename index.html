Welcome to Church

Rooted in Grace. Growing in Faith.

We exist to glorify God by leading people into an authentic, life-transforming relationship with Jesus Christ.

Biblical Teaching

Every message is rooted in the truth of God’s Word, offering verse-by-verse exposition and practical wisdom for everyday life.

Authentic Community

We are a multi-generational family walking through life together. From small groups to youth nights, you'll find genuine encouragement.

Loving Service

Faith in action: We are passionate about serving our local city through food drives, community outreach, and global missionary support.

First Time Visiting?

What to Expect on Sunday

Here is a quick look at what your first visit will be like from the moment you pull into our parking lot.

1

Warm Welcome

Our friendly hospitality team will greet you with a smile, help you find a cup of coffee, and direct you to the auditorium.

2

Uplifting Worship

Services run about 70 minutes with contemporary worship songs and classic hymns, followed by an inspiring Bible-centered message.

3

Safe Kids Ministry

Safe, clean, and fun classrooms for infants through 5th graders with background-checked volunteers who love teaching God's Word.

4

Come As You Are

There's no dress code! You’ll see everything from casual jeans and t-shirts to Sunday best. We care about you, not what you wear.
